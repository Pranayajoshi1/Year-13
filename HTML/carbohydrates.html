What Are Carbohydrates?

Carbohydtes are one of the three main macronutrients, alongsde protein and healthy fats. They are the body's pferred source of energy because they are broken down into lucose, which fuels your muscles, brain, and other orgas.

Carbohydrates are found in foods such as rice, bread, pasta, potatoes, oats, fruit, vegetables and many other everyday foods. By eting enough healthy carbohydrates helps you to perfomr during exscise and recover more effectively afterwards.

Why Are Carbohydrates Important?

Main Energy Source

Carbohydrates provide the energy your body needs for workouts, everyday activities and normal body functions.

Improves Performance

By eating enough carbohydrates helps you train harder, lift heavier weights and improve your overall performance.

Supports Recovery

Carbohydrates replace glycogen stores after exercise which helps your muscles recover faster and prepare for your next workout.

Supports Brain Function

Your brain relies on glucose from carbohydrates to stay focused, think clearly and concentrate throughout the day.

  • White Bread

  • Fruit Juice
  • Sweets (in moderation)

Carb Tips

  • Choose whole food carbohydrate sources most of the time.
  • Eat carbohydrates before and after workouts for better performance and recovery.
  • Do not completely avoid carbs because they are your body's main source of energy.
  • Choose high-fibre carbohydrates to help keep you full for longer.

How Many Carbohydrates Do You Need?

The amount of carbohydrates you need depends on your body weight, activity level and fitness goals.

A good starting point for beginners who exercise regularly is around 3–6 grams of carbohydrates per kilogram of body weight each day.

If you train harder or are trying to gain muscle, you may need more carbohydrates. If you are trying to lose fat, you can stay towards the lower end of the range while still eating enough to fuel your workouts.
