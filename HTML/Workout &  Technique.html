Choose Your Workout Split

PPL (Push Pull Legs)

In this split you can do 3-day split that trains each muscle group twice a week, which help you build strength and muscle (You can even do 6 days split with this)

Day 1 Push
Day 2 Pull
Day 3 Legs
Day 4 Rest
Day 5 Push
Day 6 Pull
Day 7 Rest

Bro Split

In This you train one main muscle group each day. It’s simple and easy to follow, especially if you like focusing on one muscle at a time. Also you can get enough time to revoever your muslce.

Day 2 Back

Day 4 Arms
