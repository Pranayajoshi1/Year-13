Workout & Technique

Train smarter. Not just harder. Here you can find the right Technique for every muscle group and follow simple plans that helps you to reach your goals.

Treadmill

Warms up your whole body and gets your heart rate up before your workout.

Leg Swings

Warms up your hips and legs

VIDEO CREDIT: MyPhysioRehab

Shoulder Pass-Throughs

Warms up your shoulders and upper body and improves shoulder mobility

Band Pull Aparts

Activates your upper back before lifting.

VIDEO CREDIT: Men's Health

Bench Press

Targets your chest, triceps, and front shoulders, mainly focusing on your chest.

Flys

Works your chest muscles and helps improve chest strength and control.

Incline dumbell press

Target your upper chest.

Lat pull down

Target your Lats

Seated row

Target Upper back + lats

T-bar row

Target Lats, traps, rhomboids

Shoulders

Targets the shoulders, mainly the front and side delts

Lateral raises

Targets the side shoulders

Triceps pushdown

Targets the triceps at the back of your upper arms.

Biceps curl

Targets the biceps

Leg press

Targets the quads, glutes, and hamstrings

Legs Extension

Targets the quadriceps (front of your thighs).

Leg curl

Targets the hamstrings

Calf Raises

Targets the calf muscles

Plank

Strengthens your core and improves stability.

Crunch

Choose Your Workout Split

PPL (Push Pull Legs)

In this split you can do 3-day split that trains each muscle group twice a week, which help you build strength and muscle (You can even do 6 days split with this)

Day 1 Push
Day 2 Pull
Day 3 Legs
Day 4 Rest
Day 5 Push
Day 6 Pull
Day 7 Rest

Bro Split

In This you train one main muscle group each day. It’s simple and easy to follow, especially if you like focusing on one muscle at a time. Also you can get enough time to revoever your muslce.

Day 1 Chest
Day 2 Back
Day 3 Shoulders
Day 4 Arms
Day 5 Legs
Day 6 Rest
Day 7 Rest

Upper Lower Split

In this split you tain your upper body one day and your lower body the next. It’s simple and easy to follow, and you can train each muscle twice or even three times a week

Day 1 Upper Body
Day 2 Lower Body
Day 3 Rest
Day 4 Upper Body
Day 5 Lower Body
Day 6 Rest
Day 7 Rest
